ABOUT

Dr Ana Grahm Potter

Dentist

At DentalSkuy, our journey in the world of dentistry began over 12 years ago when Dr. Ana Graham Potter established our practice. Since then, we have proudly served the Jakarta, ID area, providing top-notch dental care to countless families and individuals.

Over the years, our commitment to excellence and our patients' well-being has remained unwavering. We've grown and evolved, incorporating the latest dental technologies and techniques to ensure the best possible care for our valued patients.

Our Values

Patient Education Professional Excellence Compassion Transparency Patient-Centered Care Preventive Focus

Our mission at DentalSkuy is simple yet profound: to deliver exceptional dental care with compassion and expertise.

We are dedicated to improving the oral health and overall well-being of every patient who walks through our doors.

Our team is passionate about what we do, and we take pride in helping our patients achieve and maintain beautiful, healthy smiles. We believe that a confident smile is a key to a happier, more fulfilling life.
